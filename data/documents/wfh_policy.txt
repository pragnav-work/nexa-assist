NEXACORE TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.
WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Policy ID: NC-HR-002
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. ELIGIBILITY

Employees may use up to 8 Work From Home (WFH) days per calendar month.

WFH is intended for focused work, personal logistics, or temporary commuting constraints.

Certain office-based activities may require employees to work from the office.

2. WFH REQUEST RULES

Employees should request planned WFH at least 1 working day in advance.

The employee must remain reachable during normal working hours.

WFH requests should include:
- Requested date
- Short reason for working remotely

3. WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, unless the employee's team follows a different schedule.

Employees must attend required meetings and maintain normal communication while working remotely.

4. OFFICE PRESENCE

Employees may be asked to attend important meetings, workshops, onboarding activities, or team events at an office location.

5. NEXAASSIST SCOPE

NexaAssist can explain WFH rules and the monthly WFH limit.

The demo assistant does not submit WFH requests unless a dedicated WFH action tool is enabled.
