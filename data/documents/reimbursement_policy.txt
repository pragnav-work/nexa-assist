NEXACORE TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.
EMPLOYEE EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Policy ID: NC-FIN-002
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES

The following expenses may be reimbursable when approved:
- Business travel
- Client meeting expenses
- Office supplies purchased for business use
- Approved professional events

Every expense must have a clear business purpose.

2. SUBMISSION TIMELINE

Employees should submit expenses within 10 calendar days of the expense date or the end of an approved business trip.

3. RECEIPT REQUIREMENTS

Receipts or equivalent proof of purchase should be attached for reimbursable expenses.

For the NexaAssist demo project, expense records contain:
- Expense description
- Amount
- Date
- Expense type
- Status

4. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

The following are not reimbursable:
- Personal purchases
- Fines
- Penalties
- Entertainment unrelated to business activity
- Alcoholic beverages

5. EXPENSE STATUS

Pending:
The expense has been submitted and is awaiting processing.

Approved:
The expense has passed the company review process.

Rejected:
The expense was not approved for reimbursement.

Paid:
The approved reimbursement has been processed.

This document is fictional and created for the NexaAssist training/demo project.
