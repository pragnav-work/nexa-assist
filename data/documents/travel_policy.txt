NEXACORE TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.
BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Policy ID: NC-FIN-001
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. TRAVEL ELIGIBILITY

Business travel must support a legitimate company activity such as:
- Client meetings
- Conferences
- Training
- Internal business events

Employees should obtain travel confirmation before making non-refundable bookings.

2. AIR AND RAIL TRAVEL

Domestic air travel should normally use economy class.

Rail travel should normally use AC Chair Car or AC 2 Tier depending on journey duration and availability.

3. ACCOMMODATION

Hotel reimbursement is permitted for approved business trips.

The standard accommodation limit is INR 6,000 per night for domestic travel, excluding applicable taxes.

4. MEALS

Reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel may be reimbursed.

Personal entertainment and alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable under this policy.

5. EXPENSE SUBMISSION

Travel expenses should be submitted within 10 calendar days after returning from the trip.

Employees should retain receipts for reimbursable expenses.
